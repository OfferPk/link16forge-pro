# Link16Forge Pro Monetization & Marketing Playbook

## 1\. Commercial Market Landscape

Tactical Data Link 16 is the standard tactical communications backbone across NATO, the US Armed Forces, the UK Ministry of Defence, the UAE Armed Forces, and allied air forces and navies globally. Traditional hardware terminal test sets and proprietary TDL protocol analyzers (Viasat, BAE Systems, Ultra Electronics, Northrop Grumman) require \$30,000 to \$80,000 per workstation license. Link16Forge Pro delivers an accessible, browser-native development, simulation, and compliance audit environment for defense systems engineers, radar tracking architects, and C4ISR battle management software teams.
